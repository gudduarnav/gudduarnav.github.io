Research agenda

Wireless systems shaped by physics.

My research connects physically grounded channel models with practical optimization strategies for energy-efficient, secure, and resilient next-generation networks.

01 · PASS

Pinching-antenna systems

Position and beam co-design for reconfigurable waveguide-fed apertures, including multi-waveguide multicast, sustainable deployments, and covert communication.

PASS Near field Beamforming
02 · Security

Secure and energy-aware links

Physical-layer security for NOMA and ELAA systems that jointly deliver confidential information and harvested energy to zero-energy and IoT devices.

NOMA SWIPT ELAA
03 · Surfaces

RIS, SIM, and holographic design

Resource allocation for reconfigurable and stacked intelligent surfaces, with optimization across power, phase, position, reliability, and short-packet delay.

RIS / SIM HRIS Optimization
