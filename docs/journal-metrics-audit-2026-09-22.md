## Category and status decisions

- Telecommunications is the primary displayed category for the five IEEE journals. WCL is Q2 in this category despite its Q1 Electrical & Electronic Engineering classification. Other verified category ranks appear in each journal's expanded details.
- Optical and Quantum Electronics is not ranked in the JCR Telecommunications category. It is Q1 in Optics; its other 2024 categories are Electrical & Electronic Engineering, Q2, 114/368, and Quantum Science & Technology, Q2, 13/28. Clarivate Master Journal List currently reports it as **On Hold**. The site clearly identifies its metrics as historical and does not imply a 2025 JIF or current uninterrupted indexing.
- JIF and JCI apply to journals, including the journal survey and magazine records. The eight conference papers and one proceedings chapter are labeled not applicable; they are not assigned quartiles or borrowed journal metrics.
- Four distinguishable colors identify Q1–Q4, always accompanied by textual quartile labels, category names, and metric years. Only verified classifications are assigned.
- These metrics describe publication venues, not the quality of individual articles.
